# How to Get Your Website Online (Live Link)

This guide shows you how to get a shareable URL for your Spring Grove B&B website.

---

## Option 1: Netlify (Easiest - 2 Minutes)

**Recommended for beginners. No coding needed.**

1. Go to **https://www.netlify.com** and sign up for a free account (use your email or Google account).

2. On your Netlify dashboard, find the area that says **"Drag and drop your site folder here"**.

3. Open your file explorer, go to:
   ```
   Desktop > meow
   ```
   Drag the entire `meow` folder and drop it onto the Netlify page.

4. Netlify will upload your site and give you a free link instantly, like:
   ```
   https://spring-grove-bb-abc123.netlify.app
   ```

5. Copy that link and paste it into your **Google Maps Business Profile → Website field**.

**Optional:** In Netlify settings, you can rename the link to something easier like `spring-grove-bb.netlify.app`.

---

## Option 2: GitHub Pages (Free, Permanent)

Good if you want to keep updating the site over time.

1. Go to **https://github.com** and create a free account.

2. Click **"New Repository"** and name it `spring-grove-bb`.

3. Upload all files from your `meow` folder:
   - `index.html`
   - `gallery.html`
   - `css/style.css`
   - `css/gallery.css`
   - `js/main.js`
   - `js/gallery.js`

4. Go to **Settings → Pages** (left sidebar).

5. Under "Source", select:
   - Branch: `main`
   - Folder: `/ (root)`
   - Click **Save**

6. Your site will be live at:
   ```
   https://yourusername.github.io/spring-grove-bb/
   ```

---

## Option 3: Vercel (Fast & Modern)

1. Go to **https://vercel.com** and sign up with your GitHub account.

2. Click **"Add New Project"** → **"Upload"** and select your `meow` folder.

3. Vercel will deploy instantly and give you a link like:
   ```
   https://spring-grove-bb.vercel.app
   ```

---

## Option 4: Custom Domain (Most Professional)

If you want a professional link like `www.springgrovebb.com`:

1. Buy a domain from:
   - **Namecheap** (https://namecheap.com) - ~Rs. 500-800/year
   - **GoDaddy** (https://godaddy.com)

2. Use any hosting above (Netlify, Vercel, or GitHub Pages) and connect your custom domain in the settings.

---

## What Link to Put in Google Maps?

Once deployed, copy your live link (e.g., `https://spring-grove-bb.netlify.app`) and add it to:

**Google Business Profile → Edit Profile → Website**
